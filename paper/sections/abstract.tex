Deepfakes threaten media integrity and platform trust, yet many detectors that perform well on curated benchmarks exhibit notable degradation under distribution shift. For practical use, we target on\hyp device detection where latency, footprint, and privacy constraints are strict, and where missed fakes (false negatives) carry disproportionate risk. We present MobileDeepfake, a cascaded detector optimized for mobile: a lightweight MobileNetV4 Stage~1 makes high\hyp confidence decisions quickly, while uncertain samples are escalated to a higher\hyp accuracy EfficientNetV2 Stage~2. The system is trained across multiple datasets and tuned with a cost\hyp aware threshold grid search that explicitly minimizes false negatives under an escalation\hyp rate budget. We export both stages to TorchScript, apply dynamic quantization, and integrate a compact runtime for Android.

Our pipeline is reproducible end\hyp to\hyp end: data manifests and loaders enable multi\hyp source training; a difficult\hyp sample mining procedure concentrates the expert on ambiguous cases; and an automated tuning/evaluation suite generates tables and figures directly consumed by the paper. On combined validation, the expert achieves AUC~\num{0.9633} (F1~\num{0.8930}). The tuned cascade attains near\hyp zero false negatives at low escalation (e.g., low/high~=~0.05/0.55 gives FNR~\num{0.0060} with \num{1.16}\% escalation), exposing an interpretable trade\hyp off between recall and compute. We assess robustness under common perturbations (JPEG, noise, blur, brightness) and apply temperature scaling for probability calibration when thresholds transfer across datasets. Cross\hyp dataset evaluation highlights the generalization gap typical of in\hyp the\hyp wild media, reinforcing the need for calibration and domain\hyp aware tuning.

We focus on deployability rather than proposing a new backbone: the contribution is a practical, auditable methodology that couples a two\hyp stage cascade with explicit threshold controls, calibration, and a mobile export path. We release artifacts and scripts so results can be reproduced and extended (e.g., swapping the expert, adding distillation/QAT, or adopting per\hyp family thresholding for robustness). This work provides a template for balancing recall, efficiency, and maintainability in mobile deepfake detection.
