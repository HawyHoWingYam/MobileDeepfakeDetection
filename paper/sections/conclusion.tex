We presented MobileDeepfake, a two\hyp stage cascaded deepfake detector engineered for mobile deployment. A lightweight MobileNetV4 filter and an EfficientNetV2 expert are trained in a multi\hyp dataset pipeline with difficult\hyp sample mining and threshold tuning. On combined validation, the expert attains strong discrimination (AUC~\num{0.9633}, F1~\num{0.8930}). The tuned cascade achieves near\hyp perfect recall with low escalation rates (e.g., low/high~=~0.05/0.55 gives FNR~\num{0.006} at \num{1.16}\% escalation). We export both stages to TorchScript with dynamic quantization and provide an Android integration plan; exported artifacts are compact (\SI{37.3}{MB} and \SI{49.6}{MB}, Table~\ref{tab:mobile_artifacts_auto}).

Our study highlights key trade\hyp offs between false negative control and escalation rate, and between accuracy and on\hyp device latency. Future work will focus on (i) robustness stress testing and adaptive thresholding, (ii) knowledge distillation or architecture search to further shrink Stage~2 and reduce latency, and (iii) end\hyp to\hyp end on\hyp device benchmarks across diverse hardware with optional NNAPI acceleration. We release code, manifests, and scripts to facilitate replication and extension.
