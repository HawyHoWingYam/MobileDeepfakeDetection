\textbf{Environment.} Training and analysis use PyTorch~2.1+ \cite{paszke2019pytorch}, timm, Albumentations \cite{buslaev2018albumentations}, and scikit\-learn \cite{pedregosa2011sklearn}. Scripts and requirements are provided in the repository (\verb|requirements.txt|). All runs produce a timestamped directory under \verb|outputs/| with logs (\verb|log.md|), CSV metrics, summary JSON, and plots.

\textbf{Datasets.} We train on CelebDF-v2, FaceForensics, DeeperForensics, and DFDC using balanced manifests; Deepfake-Eval-2024 is held out for validation/test. Table~\ref{tab:dataset_sizes} reports split sizes. We employ equal-by-dataset sampling during Stage~1 and difficult-subset mining for Stage~3 to focus on ambiguous patterns.

\subsection*{Cross-Dataset Generalization Protocol}
We evaluate models in two regimes: (i) combined validation across training sources; (ii) per\hyp dataset validation to expose source\hyp specific gaps; and (iii) OOD validation/test on Deepfake\hyp Eval\hyp 2024. Optional leave\hyp one\hyp source\hyp out runs (train on three, test on the fourth) provide tighter estimates of generalization (protocol) \cite{wilddeepfake2021}. These setups follow common OOD practice for separating in\hyp distribution vs. out\hyp of\-distribution behavior (baseline) \cite{hendrycks2017baseline}.

\subsection*{Balanced Sampling and Ablations}
Stage~1 uses an equal\hyp by\hyp dataset \verb|WeightedRandomSampler| with per\hyp dataset weights $w_k\propto 1/n_k$. We ablate (a) no balancing, (b) equal by dataset, and (c) capped contributions per epoch. For Stage~3, hard\hyp subset training is validated on the full combined validation set to mitigate selection bias.

\subsection*{Augmentation Rationale}
Light geometric/photometric augmentation improves robustness; a small real\hyp class augmentation probability reduces asymmetry in error gradients. Additional perturbation sweeps (JPEG/noise/blur/brightness) are reported separately to avoid conflating training augmentation with robustness evaluation. We implement these augmentations with Albumentations \cite{buslaev2018albumentations} for speed and flexibility.
