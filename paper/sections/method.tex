We adopt a six-stage pipeline designed for both accuracy and deployability, mapped directly to the repository structure and scripts.

\paragraph{Stage~1: Lightweight Filter (MobileNetV4).} A compact MobileNetV4 variant (\verb|mobilenetv4_hybrid_medium|) serves as a fast binary classifier. We train with BCE on a unified multi-dataset loader that balances contributions via \verb|WeightedRandomSampler| (\verb|train_mobilenet.py:create_multi_dataset_loader|). Key defaults (\verb|configs/experiment_default.yaml|): learning rate \num{1e-4}, batch size \num{256}, epochs \num{15}, AdamW with weight decay \num{1e-4}. Albumentations \cite{buslaev2018albumentations} provides light augmentation; inputs are normalized and resized to \SI{256}{px}. The Stage~1 head outputs a single logit.

\paragraph{Stage~2: Difficult Sample Mining.} We score training data with Stage~1 (no augmentation) and label samples as \emph{difficult} if they are misclassified or lie in a predefined ambiguity band around 0.5. The script \verb|tools/create_difficult_subset.py| writes a manifest (e.g., \verb|manifests/train_difficult_subset.csv|) that concentrates Stage~2 on ambiguous patterns and corner cases.

\paragraph{Stage~3: Expert Model (EfficientNetV2-b3).} The expert is trained on the difficult subset using \verb|train_efficientnet.py| with regularization (dropout 0.3), optional label smoothing, and early stopping. We use \verb|tf_efficientnetv2_b3| in our best run (AUC~\num{0.9633}, F1~\num{0.8930} on combined validation; see \verb|outputs/stage3/run_20251030_081127/|). Validation always occurs on the full multi-dataset validation split to avoid overfitting to mined examples.

\paragraph{Stage~4: Cascade and Threshold Tuning.} We define low/high thresholds on Stage~1 fake probability $p\_1(x)$. If $p\_1(x) < \tau\_{low}$ predict real; if $p\_1(x) > \tau\_{high}$ predict fake; otherwise escalate to Stage~2 whose probability $p\_2(x)$ is thresholded at $\tau\_2$ (default 0.5 or calibrated). We grid-search $(\tau\_{low},\tau\_{high})$ using cached logits for efficiency (\verb|tools/tune_cascade_system.py|), optimizing F1 and minimizing FNR under an escalation-rate budget. Representative best settings: $(0.05, 0.55)$ yields FNR~\num{0.0060} with \num{1.16}\% escalation; $(0.03, 0.55)$ yields FNR~\num{0.0054} with \num{1.50}\% escalation.

\paragraph{Stage~5: Cross-Dataset Evaluation and Robustness.} We evaluate on Deepfake\hyp Eval\hyp 2024 and generate robustness curves under JPEG, Gaussian noise, motion blur, and brightness changes (\verb|tools/analyze_robustness.py|, \verb|tools/robustness_threshold_sweep.py|). Auto-generated LaTeX snippets include numerical tables and trade-off plots.

\paragraph{Stage~6: Mobile Deployment.} We export TorchScript models with dynamic quantization (\verb|tools/export_torchscript.py|), bundle cascade thresholds/temperature, and integrate into Android via PyTorch Mobile. Table~\ref{tab:mobile_artifacts_auto} summarizes exported artifact sizes.

\subsection*{Hard Example Mining (Formalization)}
Let $\mathcal{D}=\{(x_i, y_i)\}_{i=1}^N$ be training pairs and $p_1(x)=\sigma(f_1(x))$ be the Stage~1 fake probability. We define a margin $m(x)=\lvert p_1(x)-0.5\rvert$ and an ambiguity band $\mathcal{A}_\delta=\{x: m(x)\le \delta\}$ for $\delta\in(0,0.5)$. The \emph{hard set} $\mathcal{H}$ is
\[
\mathcal{H} = \{(x,y)\in\mathcal{D}: \mathbf{1}[\hat{y}_1(x)\ne y]\ \ \text{or}\ \ x\in\mathcal{A}_\delta\},
\]
where $\hat{y}_1(x)=\mathbf{1}[p_1(x)>\tau]$ is a provisional decision at threshold $\tau$. We optionally stratify by dataset label $d(x)$ and cap per\hyp dataset counts to avoid distributional skew. This construction is consistent with curriculum\hyp or hard\hyp example strategies \cite{bengio2009curriculum,shrivastava2016ohem}, while preserving validation on the full multi\hyp dataset split to mitigate bias.

\subsection*{Cascade Optimization (Cost-Sensitive Objective)}
Let $\tau_{\mathrm{low}}<\tau_{\mathrm{high}}$ be Stage~1 decision thresholds and $\tau_2$ the Stage~2 threshold. Define the Stage~2 escalation rate
\[ \pi_2(\tau_{\mathrm{low}},\tau_{\mathrm{high}})=\Pr\big(\tau_{\mathrm{low}}\le p_1(x)\le\tau_{\mathrm{high}}\big). \]
We optimize for low false negatives under an efficiency budget $B$:
\[
\min_{\tau_{\mathrm{low}},\tau_{\mathrm{high}},\,\tau_2}\ \mathrm{FNR}\big(\tau_{\mathrm{low}},\tau_{\mathrm{high}},\tau_2\big)\quad\text{s.t.}\quad \pi_2(\tau_{\mathrm{low}},\tau_{\mathrm{high}})\le B.
\]
An equivalent cost\hyp sensitive form uses expected compute $\mathbb{E}[C]=C_1\cdot(1-\pi_2)+C_2\cdot\pi_2\le K$ with Stage~1/2 unit costs $(C_1,C_2)$ \cite{elkan2001cost}. We implement a grid search that is simple, parallel, and reproducible; Bayesian optimization or dynamic programming are viable future accelerators \cite{snoek2012bayesopt}.

\subsection*{Routing Strategies}
We adopt confidence\hyp based routing with two thresholds $(\tau_{\mathrm{low}},\tau_{\mathrm{high}})$ for interpretability and direct control over $\pi_2$. Alternatives include entropy\hyp or margin\hyp based routing \cite{kaya2019shallowdeep,teerapittayanon2017branchynet}. Confidence routing works well with calibrated logits (optional temperature on Stage~2) and matches deployment needs where a tunable escalation rate is critical.

\subsection*{Quantization and Deployment Notes}
We use post\hyp training dynamic quantization for linear layers to reduce on\hyp device footprint and maintain accuracy. Quantization\hyp aware training (QAT) \cite{jacob2017integeronly} can further reduce the gap, but increases training complexity; we leave mixed\hyp precision (e.g., INT4+INT8) and advanced schemes as future work within the same export pipeline. Temperature scaling is used for probability calibration when transferring thresholds across datasets \cite{guo2017calibration}.

\subsection*{Objectives, Milestones, and Evaluation Strategy}
\textbf{Objectives.} Achieve a practical deepfake detector on mobile devices with: (i) high recall (low FNR) under realistic conditions; (ii) low latency and small footprint; (iii) reproducible training and tuning pipelines with auditable artifacts.

\textbf{Milestones.} The roadmap includes data preparation across multiple datasets; Stage~1 training; Stage~2 difficult-sample mining and Stage~3 expert training; cascade threshold tuning with caching; cross-dataset evaluation; and mobile export/integration. Optional enhancements include knowledge distillation (teacher--student), pruning, and advanced quantization; our pipeline can incorporate these by swapping the expert while keeping the same tuning and evaluation.

\textbf{Evaluation Strategy.} We report AUC, F1, Accuracy, Precision/Recall, FNR/FPR, and Stage~2 escalation rate for cascade analysis; for deployment, we report average per-frame latency (Stage~1/2) and application size. Robustness is assessed via controlled perturbations with performance vs. strength curves and FNR vs. Stage~2 trade-off sweeps.
